FrugalGPT (Chen, Zaharia, Zou, 2023) proposes three strategies for cutting LLM
inference cost: prompt adaptation (shrinking or rewriting prompts), LLM
approximation (using a cheaper proxy model), and LLM cascades. The cascade
approach sequentially queries models from cheapest to most expensive, using
each model's own confidence score to decide whether to accept its answer or
escalate to the next tier. On benchmark tasks, FrugalGPT matches GPT-4-level
accuracy for as little as 2% of GPT-4's cost, and can also exceed GPT-4's
accuracy by about 4% at the same total cost by combining multiple cheaper
models. The key insight is that most queries are easy enough for a small
model to answer correctly and confidently -- only genuinely hard queries need
to reach the expensive tier at all.
